How it works

  • 1. Create your event website with names, date, venue, schedule, photos and invitation card.
  • 2. Share the link, QR code or WhatsApp message with your guests.
  • 3. Go live from any phone, tablet or laptop. Guests watch on the same page, no app needed.

Good to know

  • Guests only need a phone or computer with internet. No account is needed to watch.
  • The person broadcasting needs a steady internet connection. Wi-Fi or a good mobile signal works best.
  • If a connection is weak, the video quality lowers automatically so the stream keeps playing.
  • Telugu and Hindi text is entered by you, so the page shows exactly your wording.
